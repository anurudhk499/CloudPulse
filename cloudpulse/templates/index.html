    Technical details (for your report and viva)

    Forecast accuracy (test period only)

    Everything here is a simulation with assumed values: VM capacity, start-up time, price, and a simple response-time estimate (it grows as computers fill up). The default traffic is synthetic unless you replaced data/trace.csv. Models learn from the first 70% of the trace and are tested on the last 30%. The Random Forest cannot predict traffic far above anything it saw in training, so it struggles on spikes. Results show behaviour under these assumptions, not real cloud savings.

    Guide

    The few ideas you need to follow the simulation.

    The cloudComputers you rent by the hour instead of buying.
    Virtual machine (VM)One rented computer. Each glowing block in the simulator is one VM.
    Auto-scalingRenting more VMs when busy and fewer when quiet. That is what is tested here.
    Start-up timeA new VM needs minutes to switch on, so reacting late means visitors wait or are turned away.

    The four managers

    • Fixed fleetSame number of VMs all day, sized for a busy period. Safe but wasteful.
    • Rule-basedAdds VMs once the existing ones look busy. Simple, but always a little late.
    • AI predictorA Random Forest forecasts traffic for when new VMs will be ready and orders early.
    • Perfect foresightKnows the real future. Not possible in practice; it shows the best achievable result.

    Reading the meters

    • How busy the computers areVisitors divided by capacity. Above 100% means overload.
    • Page speedA simple estimate that gets slower as VMs fill up.
    • Time overloadedShare of time when demand exceeded capacity so far.

    All figures come from a simulation with assumed values, not from a real cloud.
